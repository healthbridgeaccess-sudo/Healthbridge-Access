ABOUT US

Healthcare should be accessible to everyone.

HealthBridge Access is a student-led organization based in Orange County, California, focused on addressing barriers to affordable healthcare in our community. For people who are uninsured or unable to afford healthcare, accessing basic health services can be difficult. We believe everyone deserves access to healthcare, regardless of their financial situation.

We are currently developing HealthBridge Access with the goal of bringing free and accessible healthcare opportunities into the community. We plan to organize community events where qualified healthcare professionals and organizations can provide services such as free health screenings and other healthcare services, depending on each event.

By bringing these opportunities closer to the community, we hope to help people who may otherwise have limited access to affordable healthcare. As HealthBridge Access grows, we plan to build relationships with healthcare professionals and organizations that can help make these events possible.

Our MissionTo help make healthcare more accessible and affordable by bringing free healthcare opportunities to people in our community who may have limited access to care.
